About Me

I'm Ganna — a content creator and copywriter based in Mansoura, currently finishing my degree in Commerce, English Section, at Mansoura University.

My work sits between strategy and story. On one side: content pillars, calendars, audience and competitor research, understanding where a piece fits in the buyer journey. On the other: the actual writing — the hooks, the scripts, the copy that decides whether someone keeps scrolling or keeps reading.

I started out writing SEO-driven content, built around real search intent rather than guesswork, and that gave me a solid instinct for what an audience is actually looking for. From there, I moved into copywriting and script writing for social media — shorter, sharper, more about tone and hook than keywords.

What I enjoy most is the part in between: taking a rough idea and a brand's voice, and finding the few words that make the two fit together.
